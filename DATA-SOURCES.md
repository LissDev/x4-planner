# X4 9.0 data provenance

`data.js` is generated from the locally installed X4 Foundations 9.0.0.0 game files. The extractor reads `01.cat` through `09.cat` and their matching `.dat` files, then the `ext_*.cat`/`.dat` pairs in these official extensions only: `ego_dlc_split`, `ego_dlc_terran`, `ego_dlc_boron`, `ego_dlc_mini_01`, `ego_dlc_mini_02`, `ego_dlc_pirate`, and `ego_dlc_timelines`. Other installed extensions are excluded. The game directory is read only.

The principal game records are `libraries/wares.xml` (prices, ware recipes, workunit food and medicine demand, and module build materials), `assets/structures/production/macros/*_macro.xml` (queue method and workforce limit), `assets/structures/habitat/macros/*_macro.xml` (population capacity and race), `assets/structures/storage/macros/*_macro.xml` (cargo capacity and type), `assets/structures/dock/macros/*_macro.xml` (dock and pier identity), `assets/structures/buildmodule/macros/*_macro.xml` (ship fabrication and maintenance bays), and `t/0001-l007.xml` / `t/0001-l044.xml` (Russian and English names). The extractor overlays official XML `<diff>` additions in archive order, resolves `{page,text}` references against the game's language files, and verifies every read CAT entry against its MD5 digest. The `fingerprint` in the output is derived from the official CAT indexes. This is an offline, generated data file; the generated-files exception to the repository's test-first rule applies.

Regenerate and validate with the commands below. Replace `<X4_INSTALL_DIRECTORY>` with your X4 installation folder, keeping the quotes around the path:

```powershell
python scripts/extract-data.py --game '<X4_INSTALL_DIRECTORY>' --output data.js
python scripts/extract-data.py --game '<X4_INSTALL_DIRECTORY>' --check data.js
```

`globalThis.X4_DATA` contains `wares`, `modules`, `rawWares`, `workforceConsumption`, and `workforceIdleConsumption`. Ware prices are the game's min/average/max prices in credits. `storage` is the game's transport class and `volume` is storage volume per item. Module `buildMaterials` maps ware IDs to quantities; multiply by chosen ware prices for construction cost. Module ware prices are market prices for a module and differ from the sum of its build materials.

Production `recipes` contain inputs and outputs per cycle, `cycleSeconds`, `workforceBonus` (the game's fractional output bonus at full staffing), and `sunlightScaled`. Workforce does not increase recipe inputs. The workunit rates are units per worker per hour, derived from each race's `workunit_busy` or `workunit_idle` production: input amount × 3600 ÷ cycle seconds ÷ workers per cycle. `workforceConsumption` is the busy rate; for example, one busy Argon worker consumes 2.25 food rations and 1.35 medical supplies per hour. Solar output's sunlight factor is supplied by the calculator, since the game file supplies the base rate and marks sunlight as an effect.

Some scrap recyclers offer alternative queue products. Each output gets a distinct recipe ID of the form `moduleId#method#outputWareId`; these alternatives do not run simultaneously in one module. Modules with no player blueprint and mission-only modules are excluded, as are special empty player HQ production placeholders. Habitat modules have population `capacity` and construction materials but no recipe. Storage modules have cargo `capacity` in cubic meters and `storageType` (`container`, `solid`, `liquid`, or `condensate`); docks and piers have zero cargo capacity in this schema. The catalog also includes 18 player-blueprint `shipyard` bays with official fabrication/maintenance type, supported ship sizes, race, construction materials, and source workforce maximum. These infrastructure kinds have no hourly ware recipe and contribute to construction cost without changing production balances. Shipyard workforce maximum is source metadata; active staffing, crew supplies, ship production, and repair income are not modeled. Defence and other unsupported nonproduction modules remain outside the catalog.

Validation requires unique IDs, Russian names, ordered nonnegative prices, positive habitat and storage capacities, positive recipe cycle times, valid ware references and quantities, positive construction material sums, all six race diets, and representative Commonwealth, Terran, and Boron production and infrastructure modules. The exact source values can change after a game update; regenerate against the updated installation to refresh the fingerprint and catalog.
